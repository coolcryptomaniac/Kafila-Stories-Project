Travel with the founders

Twice a year, we go too.

A handful of departures where the founders travel with the group — same table, same jeep, same long evenings. You arrive not knowing anyone and leave with a group chat that outlives the trip.

Like-minded company

Twelve people who chose the same odd corner of the map as you.

Someone else drives

Permits, logistics and the difficult roads are ours to worry about.

You leave with photographs

We shoot as we go, and everything is shared with the group afterwards.
